Privacy Policy

Information you share

When you send a project inquiry, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, company, project type, location, budget, timeline, description and any attached reference files. Required fields are marked on the form.

How inquiries are handled

We use these details to review and respond to your inquiry. The contact form is delivered through FormSubmit.co to our team email address. FormSubmit's published documentation states that form submissions are kept in its archive for 30 days. Uploaded files are sent with the inquiry; do not include confidential or sensitive information in a message or attachment.

Questions

For questions about an inquiry you have sent, contact us at katoemmanganda1@gmail.com.
