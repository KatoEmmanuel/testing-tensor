Services

Areas of expertise Tensor Engineering Solutions delivers directly. Open a sheet for what the service covers, and where it applies.

A—01 Construction +

Ground-up construction managed from site mobilisation through to handover, with quality control built into every stage rather than checked at the end.

  • Site preparation, structural works, masonry, roofing and finishes, coordinated as one build programme.
  • Residential, commercial and mixed-use developments, including apartment and retail projects in Kampala.
  • Clear checks at each stage, from setting out and materials to workmanship, snagging and final handover.
Discuss a construction project
A—02 Civil Engineering +

Structural and civil engineering work addressing the technical logic beneath a project — foundations, drainage, earthworks and load-bearing systems.

  • Setting out, earthworks, foundations, drainage and concrete works planned around the actual site conditions.
  • Practical coordination between drawings, quantities, materials and the teams working on site.
  • Technical issues are identified early and resolved with the client and design team before they affect the programme.
Discuss a civil engineering project
A—03 Infrastructure +

Infrastructure delivery for roads, utilities and public or private works that other projects and communities depend on.

  • Site works, access roads, drainage and utility-related works that support buildings and the communities around them.
  • Delivery planned around safe access, durable construction and minimal disruption to neighbouring properties.
  • Tensor coordinates the work with the client, consultants and relevant site stakeholders from early planning to completion.
Discuss an infrastructure project
A—04 Project Management +

End-to-end project management for clients who need one accountable team coordinating design, contractors and schedule.

  • Scope, programme, procurement and site activities kept aligned from the first brief through to handover.
  • Regular communication on progress, decisions, costs and issues, so the client knows what needs attention.
  • On-site coordination of contractors, suppliers and technical teams, with quality and workmanship checked as work progresses.
Discuss project management support
A—05 Renovation & Restoration +

Careful improvement of existing buildings where the goal is to make the space work better without losing sight of the structure already in place.

  • Assessment, repair, reconfiguration and finishing works for residential, commercial and small mixed-use spaces.
  • Work planned around the existing building, access constraints, occupied areas and the client's priorities.
  • Existing conditions are reviewed before work begins, with the scope and budget agreed before construction starts.
