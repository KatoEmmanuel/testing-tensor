SHEET D—01WHO WE ARE

About Tensor Engineering Solutions

Based in Hesketh Bell Rd, Lugogo, Kampala, Tensor Engineering Solutions Company Limited provides coordinated engineering, construction and infrastructure services, combining technical expertise and practical site delivery to deliver functional, durable and cost-effective solutions.

SHEET D—02COMPANY STORY

Our story

Tensor Engineering Solutions Company Limited was built on a simple idea. Engineering should deliver more than plans it should deliver results. From our base in Lugogo, Kampala, we bring together technical expertise, practical thinking, and hands-on project delivery to turn ideas into dependable infrastructure. We are committed to doing the work right, building lasting value, and earning our clients’ trust on every project.

SHEET D—03MISSION, VISION & VALUES
Mission

To deliver safe, efficient and sustainable engineering, construction and infrastructure solutions through competent people, sound technical practice, responsible project management and strong client partnerships.

Vision

To be a trusted engineering and construction solutions company, recognised for excellence, innovation, integrity and lasting value across Uganda and the wider region.

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Innovation , Client Focus and Sustainability.
