SHEET A—05HOW WE WORK

A fixed sequence, applied to every project regardless of scale

01Discover

Understand the site, the brief and the constraints before any design work begins.

02Design

Develop the technical direction and structural approach with the client and any project consultants.

03Plan

Establish scope, sequencing, resourcing and schedule before ground is broken.

04Build

Execute against the plan, with inspection built into every stage rather than left to the end.

05Deliver

Inspect, close out documentation, and hand over a project the client can operate with confidence.

06Support

Provide ongoing support, maintenance, and technical guidance to ensure the project continues to perform reliably after handover.
